In Motion
The Residence

Andalusia, held high above the sea.

Set above Estepona on the quieter side of the New Golden Mile, the penthouse opens to a panorama that few in Andalusia can claim. To the east, the silver curve of the bay toward Marbella. To the south, the Rock of Gibraltar and the open Mediterranean beyond.

Two bedrooms. Two bathrooms. Two enormous terraces that wrap the residence in light. And what we believe to be the most beautiful infinity pool in Andalusia — a long, mirrored line of water that seems to pour straight into the sea.

Infinity Pool
The Pool

An infinity shared with the sea.

Long, low, and impossibly still — the pool draws the eye out to the water and beyond, dissolving the line between terrace and Mediterranean. At dusk, the Rock of Gibraltar appears as a dark silhouette to the south, and on the clearest days the coast of Africa traces the horizon across the Strait.

"The most beautiful infinity pool in Andalusia" — and it is.

The Terraces

Two open-air rooms in the sky.

One terrace for the morning — quiet, sun-drenched, made for slow coffee and the call of distant kestrels. The other for the long Andalusian evenings — wide enough for a long table and the company of friends as the sun drops behind the hills.

Together they frame every hour of the day with the same uninterrupted view.

The Studio

Where the day slows.

Some places are made for entertaining. This one is also made for thinking. Open the laptop on the terrace — the pool below, the agave at the rail, the slow turning of the Andalusian sky — and the world begins to quiet down.

There is a particular light here in the late afternoon, long and gold and unhurried, that seems to loosen the mind. Writers come to write. Painters to paint. Most of us come to remember what we were thinking about before the world got loud.

Inside & Out

Light, water, stone.

A residence designed around its view — every interior decision deferring to the sea, the sky, and the line where they meet.

The Setting

A quiet edge of the coast, minutes from everything that matters.

Estepona
10 min · 8 km

The old town — flower-draped lanes, a working fishing port, and one of the most charming centres on the Costa del Sol. The Orchidarium and the marina are minutes away.

Marbella
25 min · 25 km

Puerto Banús, the Golden Mile, the old town, Nikki Beach. The full theatre of the coast — close enough to dip into, far enough to leave behind.

Gibraltar
45 min · 50 km

The Rock — visible from the terrace, and an easy drive along the coast. Duty-free, cable car, and a quick step onto a British peninsula.

Málaga Airport
50 min · 75 km

Direct connections across Europe and beyond. The toll motorway makes the run straightforward in any season.
