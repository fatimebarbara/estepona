The Practicalities

How everything works

🔑Check-in, keys & the garage▾

For a self check-in, the lockboxes are behind the reception, under the Oceana label — we'll send you the code on the day of your arrival. If we're welcoming you in person, we'll simply hand you the keys.

The plastic key opens the garage. Past the Oceana Views reception, the entrance slopes down — just before the garage door, there's a slot on the left for the garage key. Please park only in space 14 — all the other spaces are privately owned by neighbours.

The lift is right next to the parking space, in stairwell (escalera) 6, and takes you straight up to the apartment — second floor, 2B. Arriving on foot? The ordinary key opens both the street door and the building entrance.

🅿️The parking barrier▾

If the barrier is closed, the little key on the keychain opens it.

🔢The door code▾

We'll send your code on the day of arrival. Type it on the keypad and the door opens. When you leave, press the arrow key — that's what locks the door behind you.

From inside, press the lower part of the lock to secure the door.
